# Future AI-authorship assessment product specification

**Proposal only — not implemented or approved.** The current public application provides CV quality feedback, not AI-authorship assessment. The isolated experimental model is unvalidated, always inconclusive and must not be exposed publicly. This document defines a possible future experience only if independent validation, privacy/security, legal/ethical and launch reviews all grant approval. No hiring or other consequential decision should be automated from such an assessment.

## Intended flow

1. **Upload:** Explain intended use, limitations and data handling before the user chooses a PDF/DOCX. Use explicit authorization and a clear privacy notice; avoid third-party transfers unless specifically authorized and reviewed.
2. **Extract:** Show an editable or reviewable extraction preview, file limitations and extraction errors. An empty, badly parsed or unsupported document must not produce an authorship conclusion.
3. **Assessment:** Only after a gated, approved request, present the validated research-backed assessment within its tested scope. **Inconclusive** must be a prominent first-class outcome for insufficient text, out-of-scope content, uncertainty, unsupported language or other predefined reasons. Do not force a human/AI label. Users should be able to proceed without an assessment.
4. **Evidence and uncertainty:** Explain what the system actually measures, the conditions under which it was evaluated, relevant false-positive risk and the limitations of applying aggregate findings to an individual. Generic wording, polished tone, formatting or a model score is not proof of AI use or authorship. Provide accessible explanations, errors and an avenue to challenge or correct a result; do not identify passages as proof of authorship.
5. **Optional CV quality report:** Offer the existing rule-based quality feedback separately. Do not conflate CV quality, hiring suitability, ATS certification, writing suggestions and authorship. The quality report remains optional and must not depend on an authorship result.

## Score and communication rules

Never claim a **percentage of text generated by AI**, a percentage of a document written by a person, or certainty of authorship from a classifier score. Raw TF-IDF/logistic/SVM decision values are uncalibrated margins, not probabilities. A calibrated estimate, if ever independently calibrated and validated for the intended CV population and use, is an estimate of a defined model event under specified conditions—not proof of who wrote a document. Whether and how to display any estimate requires separate product, safety and legal approval; calibration alone does not authorize a public percentage. Do not imply that a low score proves human authorship or that abstention establishes safety.

User-facing copy must state the assessed scope, possible false positives and false negatives, difficulty with short or edited CVs, and disproportionate risks under language, accessibility and demographic shifts. Any reported aggregate error rates must come from relevant independent studies with denominators, uncertainty and limits; never reuse fictional smoke-test metrics. Preserve a genuine inconclusive option and a path to human review and correction. Do not deploy for applicant selection or make accusations.

## Privacy, retention and deletion expectations

Before use, disclose the processing location, recipients, retention duration, logging, training use, access and deletion procedure. Do not use uploaded CVs for model training without separate informed permission and a separately approved pipeline. Minimize data and logs, restrict access, encrypt appropriately, avoid unnecessary storage, and specify what deletion covers—including backups and derivative artifacts—and its limits. Distinguish planned policy from current behavior: the existing application processes documents in memory and does not intentionally save them; browser or infrastructure retention remains possible. Establish and test any future deletion process before making a user-facing promise.

## Launch gates

Keep the feature off by default and unavailable in the public UI and API until an explicit independently reviewed launch decision. Required evidence includes provenance-verified and appropriately consented CV-specific independent tests; prespecified false-positive and false-negative, abstention and coverage criteria; representative subgroup and shift evaluations with uncertainty; external replication; calibration evidence if estimates are proposed; security, accessibility, privacy, legal and ethical review; a human-review/appeal process; and a monitoring, rollback and incident plan. Sample counts or a readiness script alone are not approval. Reassess on model or population changes. The present decision remains **no-go**.
