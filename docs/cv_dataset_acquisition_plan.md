# CV-authorship data acquisition and validation readiness

**Planning only. No recruitment, collection, model training on real data, or launch is approved by this document.** The current classifier is unvalidated. MAGE, HC3 and TXD-22 remain blocked. Draft participant materials require ethics, legal, privacy and security review and study-specific completion before use. No public collection endpoint exists.

## Recruitment and collection

1. Appoint a study owner and independent reviewer; approve research question, budget, sampling and compensation, security controls, consent, retention and withdrawal before approaching anyone. Recruit volunteers through opt-in professional, educational and community networks, aiming for diverse career stages, occupations, language backgrounds, accessibility needs, education and CV formats. No employer or recruiter should access individual participation decisions. Avoid coercion; compensate time fairly regardless of writing style or outcomes.
2. Give contributors **fictional candidate profiles** with invented experience and ask them to write CV-style material independently under an explained, auditable workflow. Do not request their real CV, contact details or career history. Document their attestation, permitted tools, draft/revision evidence and reviewer uncertainty. Attestation alone does not prove absence of assistance.
3. In a separately authorized process, generate fictional-profile CVs using approved local or otherwise specifically approved models only after rights, provider terms and privacy review. Record model family, version if known, dates, prompt/profile family, generation settings, and provenance evidence. Do not send participant text or personal information to external model services without separate, informed authorization and approved safeguards. No generation is performed by this plan.
4. With explicit derivative-use consent, ask for human edits of permitted generated drafts and, where specifically authorized, AI-assisted revisions of human-authored fictional text. Preserve source/variant links and document the assistance, editing instructions and transformation history. Treat `mixed` and `unknown` separately; do not infer ground truth from polished wording or silently force a binary label.
5. Independent reviewers reconcile provenance, participant consent scope, source and model-output rights, privacy review and approved use for each record. Any uncertainty remains unknown or excluded, not relabeled human. Record pseudonymous IDs only; keep consent and source evidence separately from research text. Never put real CVs in this repository.

## Permission, withdrawal and retention

Participants must be informed that the proposed study may train and evaluate research models, subject to separate consent choices and approved scope. Ask only for invented-profile text and necessary provenance; never request names, contact information, addresses or unnecessary personal history in examples. Use encrypted access-controlled storage outside Git, separate consent linkage, least-privilege roles, access logging, retention deadlines, backup expiry and incident procedures. Pseudonymization and redaction do not guarantee anonymity. Do not publish raw text or linkable hashes without a separate permission and review.

The consent form must identify a withdrawal contact and cutoff date **before** collection. On verified request, stop use; locate source and derived variants through the secure register, remove eligible records and working copies, address backups/recipients under the approved schedule, and review whether reports or models must be withdrawn or retrained. Explain practical limits once material is aggregated, deleted, shared or trained into models. Verify and document actions without storing withdrawn text. See [collection protocol](cv_authorship_data_collection.md) and the draft [participant information](research_participants/participant_information.md) and [consent form](research_participants/consent_form.md).

## Phased sample-size planning

Numbers below are **planning ranges**, not acceptance thresholds or promises of accuracy. One author/profile/prompt family may produce multiple variants; variants are not independent people. A statistician and independent reviewers must preregister targets, confidence interval widths, sampling, costs, stopping rules, missingness and subgroup analyses before collection.

| Phase | Indicative independent units | Purpose and stop/go |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Feasibility | 20–40 consenting human contributors with distinct fictional profiles, plus separate permitted model/prompt families and paired variants | Test instructions, consent, provenance, withdrawal, de-identification and operational costs. No detector validity claim. Revise protocol before expanding. |
| Development | Roughly 100–300 independent human contributors, with independently assigned profiles and a diverse approved generation pool; reserve separate validation groups | Explore data quality, model/threshold choices and preplanned error analysis. Counts are recruitment goals, not sufficient guarantees; expand only with oversight and resources. |
| Locked external evaluation | Separately recruited, independently collected human authors and distinct profiles, with unseen model/prompt families. Plan at least hundreds of independent human examples and enough AI/mixed examples to support prespecified estimates | Freeze test and selection first. Determine exact target from error-rate precision, expected abstention, clustering and subgroup needs; do not reuse development participants. |

For illustration only, with independent Bernoulli trials and zero observed false positives, the approximate one-sided 95% upper bound is `3/n`: 300 independent classified human examples still permit an upper bound near 1%; 3,000 near 0.1%. This approximation does not account for selection, correlated variants, abstentions, dataset shift, uncertainty in ground truth, or subgroup comparisons. More examples are required when errors occur, coverage is low, clusters are correlated, or subgroup estimates are planned. Use exact/Wilson or cluster-aware confidence intervals as appropriate and document assumptions. A minimum count or narrow interval cannot establish safety, fairness or a go decision.

## Independence and evaluation plan

Build connected groups for author/consent, source document, fictional profile, prompt/template family, edits and known shared origins. Split those groups before fitting; independently audit hashes, near-duplicates and shared passages. A model family is not the same thing as a model version, and different prompts or profiles may still be related. Document model-family and author holdouts separately; if constraints yield too few independent groups, redesign collection rather than breaking groups. Maintain a sealed external test with new contributors, profiles and collection site/time where feasible, unseen model families and prompt families; preserve a separate documented test registry. Automated matching cannot prove independence.

Prespecify evaluation of unseen human authors; unseen AI model and prompt families; human-edited and AI-assisted text; short/long sections; PDF/DOCX extraction, layout and reading-order differences; occupations, education and language backgrounds; and formatting and writing styles. Report human false positives, AI false negatives, class denominators, abstention and coverage, errors among classified cases and all eligible cases, and mixed/unknown prediction distributions. Confidence intervals should reflect independent units and clustered variants; suppress or clearly flag underpowered groups, and collect sensitive subgroup data only with specific consent and security justification. Examine extraction failures separately rather than treating missing text as authorship evidence.

Select models, preprocessing and thresholds **only on training/validation** under a preregistered plan. Lock them before one independent final test; never tune on the final test. Repeated test inspection requires a new holdout. Separately evaluate representative, independently consented real-world CVs only after appropriate permission and privacy protections. Fictional or entirely generated examples cannot validate behavior on real people, who may use assistance, accessibility tools, editing and different formats in unobserved ways. Even a successful study would not prove individual authorship or justify automated hiring decisions.

## Readiness software test

From `backend`, after installing the ML requirements:

```powershell
python -m ml.dataset_readiness
```

The command checks only bundled fictional metadata and prints aggregate JSON, not text. It never trains or downloads. The reusable `readiness(records, evidence=..., study=...)` function accepts Day 16/17-style manifests and optional reviewer attestations. Reported consent references and connected components are **proxies**, not verified unique authors. Model-family counts require a supplied `study.model_families` mapping; a model ID is not automatically treated as a family. The optional `independence`, `external_holdout` and `subgroup_evaluation` flags describe reviewer attestations only and never establish proof or grant approval. An external holdout attestation must identify test-record IDs and claim external collection, CV relevance, unseen authors, sealing and independent review; the tool cannot verify those claims. Details are in the code and tests. It reports `blocked_pending_independent_review` for training/evaluation and `blocked` for public release even if input metadata passes mechanical checks. Outputs can contain sensitive aggregate statistics; keep real-data reports in approved secure storage.
