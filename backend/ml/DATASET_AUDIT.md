# Fictional dataset audit

Neither fixture has verified real-world authorship labels. The smoke data and challenge data are fictional CV-style text with illustrative labels, not records of actual human, model, or editing provenance. The fictional editing description is a scenario, not a documented editing history. No real personal CVs are included. A verified label would require independently documented source and creation history; none is claimed here.

The smoke fixture contains 12 records: training 4 human / 4 AI, test 2 human / 2 AI, each with a separate fictional source and document group. Human-labeled training examples describe concrete local activities, learning, coursework, tools and volunteering. AI-labeled examples use abstract corporate phrases such as “strategic initiatives”, “innovative methodologies”, and “operational excellence”. The held-out examples repeat this contrast. Headings also vary (profile/summary versus education/experience), and length, punctuation, sentence structure, specificity, tone and perceived polish can all correlate with labels. The model's word-token TF-IDF representation can exploit vocabulary and headings rather than authorship; punctuation and formatting are largely discarded, and case is lowered. These are audit observations and hypotheses, not causal proof. Four test records cannot validate generalization.

The separate challenge has 10 original fictional records, 5 per illustrative label, with one per class in each of five categories. It deliberately reverses some smoke stereotypes. Three related rewrites (paraphrase, formatting, and imagined human-edited AI-style text) are paired to originals by source ID; they are not extra independent observations or included in the confusion matrix. All source identifiers are fictional. The challenge was constructed knowing the smoke dataset and is not a representative or independent population sample. It is not used to fit the model or select hyperparameters; repeated inspection or adaptation to it would eventually overfit the diagnostic too.

Before evaluation, exact and normalized duplicates, shared source/document identifiers, matching long lines or ten-word passages, and high character similarity are checked across the complete smoke set and challenge originals and rewrites. This is conservative, may flag boilerplate, and cannot reliably detect semantic paraphrases, translations, or hidden source relationships. Manual review and trustworthy source history remain necessary. No automated check proves independence.
