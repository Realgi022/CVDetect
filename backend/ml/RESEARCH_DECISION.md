# Research decision: no-go for public AI-writing claims

**Decision:** do not integrate this classifier into the public app, API, dashboard, or hiring workflow. Do not imply its output proves authorship. The 8-record fictional training partition and 4-record smoke test reflect assigned style stereotypes, not verified authorship. The 10-record fictional challenge, constructed with knowledge of the smoke fixture, has two human-label false positives and two AI-label false negatives. These observations expose weaknesses but estimate neither real-world accuracy nor fairness. Three paired rewrites are not independent observations. Repeated use of this challenge for design contaminates it for future selection.

The coefficient report associates words including “innovative” with the fictional AI label and “python” and “data” with the fictional human label; even common words such as “to” and “for” are associated. They reflect this tiny training set and TF-IDF normalization, not authorship or a causal explanation of any particular prediction. Specific, natural-sounding AI-labeled text and polished or career-change human-labeled text were misclassified. Different vocabularies, sparse overlap and the intercept may contribute; this experiment cannot establish which caused an individual error.

Abstention on uncalibrated decision-score magnitude is a diagnostic, not a deployment recommendation. For example, at margin 0.1, only 5/10 examples are classified and 3/5 human-labeled examples are abstained; 0/2 remaining human examples are false positives. That conditional zero does not mean the underlying model became reliable. No margin was selected on the challenge; abstention cannot repair distribution shift or validate labels.

## Future decision gates

No universal false-positive threshold guarantees safety. Before any future collection or evaluation, the accountable governance body and affected stakeholders must define an actual use case, harm model, confidence level, target population, sampling and analysis plan, and risk limits. The following **illustrative study design targets**, not approved deployment thresholds, show how criteria can be measurable:

1. Independently consent and verify provenance under [DATASET_PROTOCOL.md](DATASET_PROTOCOL.md). Pre-register model, metric definitions, abstention behavior, acceptance limits and subgroup analyses before touching a sealed independent test. Document all excluded, unknown and mixed cases and evaluate them separately; secure independent review and replicate at a separate site or time.
2. As a sample-size planning example, require at least 300 independent verified human-only source groups in the primary test and compute a one-sided 95% exact binomial upper confidence bound for the human false-positive rate. Even zero errors in 300 independent samples has an upper bound of about 0.994%; any actual acceptance limit must be justified in advance by context and harms. More samples are required for tighter bounds or if groups are dependent; cluster-aware intervals and sampling weights may be needed. Report raw counts and all intervals, not just a point estimate.
3. Predefine sufficiently represented language-background, writing-style, career-stage, length and section analyses, with minimum independent human group counts and uncertainty intervals. As an illustration, 100 independent human groups with zero errors still yields an approximately 2.95% one-sided 95% upper bound; groups below a prespecified precision requirement are inconclusive, not presumed safe. Address multiplicity, missing consent and privacy-preserving small-cell reporting. Measure error and abstention disparities, including coverage by subgroup, against prespecified justified bounds.
4. Report full confusion matrices, human false positives with denominators, AI-label false negatives, coverage, selective accuracy and abstentions by provenance and subgroup. Select any margin on validation only and freeze it before independent test; assess both non-abstained error and outcomes for everyone abstained. Specify minimum coverage and limits on both error and disparity before testing. No marginal improvement alone satisfies a safety case.
5. Require independently replicated performance under time, source, prompt/template, language and model-version shifts; independent privacy, legal, accessibility and ethics review; meaningful human oversight, redress and monitoring plans. Failure, insufficient evidence or unmeasurable harm means no-go. Meeting numerical targets would not itself prove authorship or justify automated employment decisions, and may still lead to no-go.

Alternative product research can focus on user-controlled writing assistance: highlight vague claims or opportunities for specificity without inventing facts, flag repetition, suggest clarity improvements, and provide actionable section guidance. These must be evaluated for usefulness and accessibility, avoid penalizing unfamiliar styles or requiring fabricated metrics, and make no authorship claims. Existing quality-scoring heuristics remain separate and limited.
