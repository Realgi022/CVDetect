# Future provenance-verified collection protocol (proposal)

This is a research plan, not a claim that the current binary schema supports mixed authorship or that reliable detection is possible. The current fictional fixtures have assigned labels, not verified histories. They test code and known style contrasts only; they cannot estimate population accuracy, fairness, or authorship. Do not collect real CVs for this repository.

## Governance and collection

Before collection, specify the research question, intended population, anticipated harms, recruitment and sampling plan, budget, retention schedule, independent oversight and analysis plan. Obtain informed, freely given, revocable consent appropriate to the study; explain uses, sharing, withdrawal limitations after aggregation or deletion, and that participation cannot affect employment. Recruit beyond convenience sources. Avoid coercion and any hiring evaluation. Use CV-style tasks about invented roles and accomplishments wherever feasible, rather than actual applicant CVs. Do not solicit names, contact details, employer names, precise locations, dates, or other unnecessary identifiers. Review for unintended sensitive content before inclusion; removal can alter text and must be documented. Pseudonymization is not anonymization.

Store consent records and identity linkage separately from encrypted study text, restrict access by role, audit access, encrypt transfers and backups, define incident response, and prohibit public release or Git commits without separate review and consent. Set a concrete, approved retention deadline before collecting, provide a withdrawal/deletion process covering backups where feasible, and verify deletion. Follow applicable law and ethics requirements; the ignored local private-data folder is not a security boundary.

## Provenance and labels

- For human-only writing, use a consented, supervised or otherwise auditable creation workflow with timestamped draft and revision history, documented tool restrictions, and participant attestation. Independent reviewers should check the evidence; attestations alone are not proof and surveillance must be proportionate and consensual. Preserve uncertainty and mark unverifiable cases unknown.
- For model-generated writing, preserve authorized generation logs: provider/model and version, generation date, prompt and template IDs, settings, output, and any subsequent edits. Verify logs against the captured output with integrity hashes and independent review. Do not include secrets or unnecessary personal information in logs. Model access is a future study requirement, not a dependency of this experiment.
- Record creation stages and spans where feasible: human-originated, model-originated, AI-assisted human writing, human-edited AI output, iterative mixed writing, and unknown. Keep original drafts and edits linked, with consent and retention controls. Treat assistance types and edit intensity as separate provenance attributes, not a forced binary truth; exclude uncertain/mixed cases from a strictly defined binary primary analysis and report them separately as an important diagnostic. Establish adjudication and disagreement procedures and never infer provenance solely from writing style.

## Independence and leakage controls

Assign stable pseudonymous author, generator/version, prompt/template family, source and document/revision IDs. Build connected components for shared authors, source documents, revisions, paraphrases, and any other known shared origin; split components, not individual fragments. Ensure train, validation and sealed test do not share authors, document groups or revisions. Predefine and audit disjoint prompt/template families across partitions; shared generic boilerplate must be removed or explicitly tracked and evaluated, not silently treated as independence. Where feasible also hold out generator versions and collection sites/time periods for shift testing; report any overlap. If overlapping constraints produce one connected component or insufficient data, redesign collection rather than break groups.

Scan within and across partitions for exact, Unicode/case/punctuation-normalized and passage duplicates, and review near matches manually, including paraphrases and translations. The existing checks are not semantic deduplication, cannot discover undocumented source relationships, and may flag common boilerplate. Record decisions and investigate shared templates, prompt reuse, related authors and editing histories. Lock the test split and analysis plan before model selection; use training only to fit preprocessing and parameters, validation only for development and any abstention threshold, and an independently curated sealed test once for final evaluation. Repeated test inspection contaminates it; use a new independent holdout for later claims.

## Evaluation design

Predefine representative sampling, independent source counts, outcomes, confidence intervals, missing-data rules, and separate reporting for writing styles, career stages, language backgrounds, CV sections and text lengths. Recruit enough independent groups for each planned analysis; disclose unmeasurable or underpowered groups rather than suppressing them or treating related revisions as independent. Use sensitive or self-reported attributes only with explicit consent, strong safeguards and a justified fairness-audit purpose, never as classifier features; proxies and self-selection can still cause harm. Report false positives, false negatives, abstention and coverage overall and across adequately sized groups, including mixed/unknown cases separately. Evaluate shifts and accessibility-related variation without treating a writing style as a deficit.
